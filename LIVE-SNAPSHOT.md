This branch holds what is live on tuvi.terranimus.com/chua (and /temple, /tera, /sawon, /simiao, /mandir, /khram) as of

## 4 Nov 2026: Vietnamese temple soundscape, sound on the first touch

The owner: "Cần thêm nhạc tự bật, cho giống chùa Việt Nam". Same patch method (Darren's Worker modules unchanged,
etag 7050dc2951af; only index.html and the CSP hashes changed). All sound is generated by the page; no recording:
- Sound starts on the first touch or key anywhere on the page (before entering too; browsers forbid sound before
  that), with a 🔊/🔇 switch on the entry card. Leaving the hall stops it; the next touch starts it again.
- "Không gian chùa" under every channel: the great bell (đại hồng chung) struck once or three times about every
  minute (the Buddha's halo pulses with it; it replaces the old 45-second synth bell), wind chimes, light wind, birds.
- The Đàn tranh channel is now "Đàn tranh · sáo trúc": bamboo-flute phrases (breath, glides, growing vibrato)
  between the plucked phrases.
